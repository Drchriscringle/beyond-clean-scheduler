How it works

A rating for every player

Every player has a rating that rises when they win and falls when they lose. Beating a strong player counts for more. Each player also has a separate rating for hard courts, clay and grass, and a forecast uses both.

From ratings to sets

The gap between two ratings gives the chance of winning. From that we work out the chance of each set score, best of three or best of five.

Checked in public

Every forecast is made before the match, using only earlier results. Then it’s checked against what happened, misses included.
