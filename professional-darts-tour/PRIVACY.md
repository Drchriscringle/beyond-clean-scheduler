# Privacy Policy: Professional Darts Tour

_Last updated: 1 October 2026_

Professional Darts Tour ("the app") is a darts career game. This policy explains what
happens to your information when you use it. The short version: **the app does not collect,
store or share any personal data on our servers. Everything stays on your device.**

## Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your phone or tablet only:

- your career save (player name, nickname, nation, age, results, rankings, settings, shirt and
  face design);
- practice and pub-mode records;
- automatic backup copies of your career.

This information never leaves your device unless you choose to export it (see below). It is
deleted when you use **Delete career** in Settings or uninstall the app.

## Microphone and voice scoring

Voice scoring is optional. The microphone is used only while you tap **Say score**, or while
hands-free voice scoring is switched on in Settings during your turn in a match.

Your speech is turned into text by your device's own speech recognition service (Apple Speech
on iPhone and iPad, Google speech services on Android, or your browser's speech service in the
web version). Depending on your device and settings, those services may process the audio on
Apple's or Google's servers under their own privacy policies. The app only receives the text
(for example "one hundred and forty") to fill in your score. It does not record, keep or send
audio anywhere.

## Spoken commentary

The match caller uses your device's built-in text-to-speech voices. No information is sent
anywhere for this.

## Exporting a save file

If you choose **Export save file**, the app creates a file of your career and opens your
device's share sheet (or downloads it in a browser). You decide where it goes, for example
email, Files, iCloud Drive or Google Drive. Loading a save file reads only the file you pick.

## What the app does not do

- No account or sign-in.
- No advertising.
- No analytics, tracking or third-party data-collection code.
- No location, contacts, photos or camera access.
- No in-app purchases.
- The app works offline. It does not need an internet connection to play.

## Web version

If you play the web version in a browser, the page is served by our hosting provider
(Netlify), which may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for
security and reliability. Your game data is still stored only in your browser.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not collect personal information
from anyone.
